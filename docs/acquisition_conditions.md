# Acquisition conditions

The real images were obtained during routine monitoring at the Research
Institute for Aquaculture No. 1 in Hai Phong, Viet Nam, from 30 August to
19 September 2025. Two fixed Barlus IPC5MPIR-BX10 underwater cameras observed
golden pompano (*Trachinotus blochii*) in shallow cement rearing tanks. Each
camera was housed in IP68-rated 304 stainless steel with a tempered-glass
viewport, used a 3.6 mm lens with an approximately 85° horizontal field of
view, and was mounted at approximately 1 m depth.

Recordings used ambient daylight. The routine schedule comprised one morning
session at approximately 09:00 and one afternoon session at approximately
16:00, each lasting about one hour. The source videos and extracted images
have the same resolution, 3072 × 2048 pixels. Frames were sampled every 10 s
from approximately 53 h of valid footage.

Instrumental measurements of water temperature, salinity, turbidity,
suspended solids, chlorophyll, illumination, and water chemistry were not
collected with the image release. These variables should therefore not be
inferred from CleanCam labels.
